# Paper Analysis

Choose the reading depth from the user's purpose:

- **Triage:** establish relevance from title, abstract, conclusion, and key figures.
- **Targeted:** examine the research question, contribution, method, and decisive results.
- **Deep:** evaluate assumptions, derivations, experimental design, evidence, and limitations.

## Analysis contract

Separate statements made by the authors from your own inference. Record the
problem, claimed contribution, method, datasets, baselines, metrics, main
results, limitations, reproducibility information, and relationship to prior
work. Check whether the evidence actually supports the central claims and note
missing controls or ambiguous comparisons.

Use page, section, figure, or table locations when the source makes them
available. Do not invent absent experimental details. End with unanswered
questions and concrete implications for the user's work.
The [Paper Reading guide](references/README.md) is
optional background rather than a required output format.
