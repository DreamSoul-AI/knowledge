# weekly-meeting-background

Reuse the user's adopted meeting-background skill to prepare the author's summary first, followed by previous-week progress and the current-week plan. Group the opening summary by actual department or work category, explaining priorities, relationships and supported status before the detailed lists. This definition creates no scheduled task.

Fill the sources, dates and local output in [config.example.yaml](config.example.yaml), then manually run [prompt.md](prompt.md). Check daily coverage and the TODO revision, and continue the same weekly draft without duplication. Cloud updates are separate from local drafting and require an identified current document, author section and applicable authorization.

Read the author's existing section from the same meeting series before drafting. Follow its categories and use concrete item names or evidenced short results rather than prefixing every item with generic progress verbs. Preserve meaningful distinctions such as drafting versus reviewing or confirmed completion; daily checks do not establish project completion, and old plans do not resume paused work.

Use plans valid at the meeting-preparation point. A Monday replay must not backfill a later TODO into that date. Use an H1 for the author's name and H2 headings for the summary, previous week (date range), and current week (plan start), in that order. In the summary, use one paragraph per actual department or category, starting with a bold category name with punctuation outside the emphasis. Separate points inline with `1)`, `2)` and plain-text labels, without italics or a separate numbered list. Explain priorities and connections rather than mechanically repeating the lists. Place internal research under R&D. This automation omits external collaboration from all three sections unless explicitly requested for the current run. Use compact nested bullet lists for the previous and current weeks, without blank lines between parent and child items or adjacent items. Preserve necessary paragraph boundaries without creating empty text blocks in Feishu. Put source checks and trial notes in a separate record rather than appending a historical paused-items list.

Before deployment, confirm schedule, time zone and carrier chat, and verify the runtime can read the definition and complete adopted skill. Keep personal configuration and receipts in the user's environment; an empty schedule does not enable anything.
